# Synapse

**Synapse** is an AI-powered soft skills platform designed to help users assess, practice, and improve their communication and professional skills. Users can practice real-world scenarios with an AI agent, receive personalized feedback, discover courses based on their weaknesses, and connect with a community focused on continuous learning and personal development.

## Key Features

* **AI Soft Skills Coach** – Practice realistic scenarios and receive instant AI feedback.
* **Personalized Assessment** – Identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
* **Scenario-Based Practice** – Train for interviews, presentations, discussions, public speaking, and more.
* **Personalized Courses** – Get course recommendations based on your performance and learning needs.
* **Human Evaluation** – Option to receive professional feedback from real human evaluators.
* **Progress Tracking** – Monitor your performance and improvement over time.
* **Community** – Share posts, blogs, reels, experiences, and advice with other learners.
* **Continuous Improvement** – Follow a cycle of **Practice → Evaluate → Learn → Practice Again**.
